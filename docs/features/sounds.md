# Sounds

Moved from CLAUDE.md section 5. Update this file when the feature changes.

**Sounds** (Practise tab row "Sounds", Chinese tones and Japanese pitch accent; `soundsWords`, `mkSoundQ`, `startSounds`, `pySyllables`, `pyMark`, `toneSVG` at the end of js/sections.js; `view.s="review"` with `view.sounds=true`): up to 10 questions. The device voice says a learned word and the learner chooses its tones from 4 options, each drawn as a static line (one short line per syllable: tone 1 flat and high, 2 rising, 3 dip, 4 falling, light tone a short low dash) with the pinyin under it. The tones come from the tone marks of `w.r`; all 1200 Chinese words split correctly (tested). Words with 一 or 不, and words with two third tones in a row, are left out because their spoken tones differ from the written ones. It uses the `gram` question type (`q.sound=true`): answers are not added to Mistakes and do not change word review timing. **Japanese** (`mkPitchQ`, `pitchSVG`, `pitchLevels`, `accentOf`, `moraList`): the same question with the pitch pattern drawn as dots on a line over the moras, high or low, with a hollow dot for a following particle (の); options are the accent numbers 0 to the mora count (flat, head high, middle high, tail high), at most 4. Only learned words with one accent number and 2 to 6 moras are used. The accent numbers are in `ACCENT_JA` (content row `meta:accent`), 2115 of 2250 words, written by `node tools/add-accent.js` from the Kanjium database (CC BY-SA 4.0, credit in Settings); only exact matches of word and reading are used, because a match by reading alone gave wrong pitch for common words (ありがとう, おはよう). Words without accent data are skipped. Research: docs/research/learning-methods.md, proposal 1.
