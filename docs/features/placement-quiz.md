# Placement quiz

Moved from CLAUDE.md section 5. Update this file when the feature changes.

**Placement quiz** (`startPlacement`, `screenPlace`, `placeUpdate`, `placeProbe`, `placeDone`, `placeFinish`, `PLACE` in js/lessons.js; `view.s="place"`): an adaptive search, not one theme after another (before: 2 questions per theme, up to 248 questions for Chinese). `view.post[s]` is the probability that the learner knows the first s themes. Each question is a meaning question for one word of the theme at the median of that probability, so each answer halves the range. Likelihoods per answer (known / unknown theme): correct 0.9 / 0.16, "I do not know" 0.03 / 0.42, wrong 0.07 / 0.42. It stops after at least 6 questions when 90% of the probability lies within 7% of the themes (at least 10), after 20 questions, or when the learner presses "Finish now" (shown on every question). The starting theme is the 10th percentile (errs towards easier themes); the result shows the 90% range and the level. Themes before it are marked as learned with box 3 and due dates spread over 28 days. Simulated learners (honest answers) need 6 to 19 questions, 10 on average. Research: docs/research/placement-test.md.
