# Placement quiz

Moved from CLAUDE.md section 5. Update this file when the feature changes.

**Placement quiz** (`startPlacement`, `placeState`, `screenPlace`, `placeUpdate`, `placeProbe`, `placeSentProbe`, `placeDone`, `placeFinish`, `placeOpenAt`, `PLACE` in js/lessons.js; `view.s="place"`): an adaptive search in two parts, not one theme after another (before: 2 questions per theme, up to 248 questions for Chinese). At most 14 questions in total, about 12 on average. Test: tests/placement.test.js. Research: docs/research/placement-test.md.

1. **Part 1, words** (`view.part=1`). `view.post[s]` is the probability that the learner knows the first s themes. Each question is a meaning question (`mkQ("meaning")`) for one word of the theme at the median of that probability, so each answer halves the range. At least 4 and at most 8 questions (`PLACE.min`, `PLACE.maxW`). It stops early when 90% of the probability (5th to 95th percentile) lies within 7% of the themes (at least 10).
2. **Part 2, sentences** (`view.part=2`). `view.postS[s]` is the same probability for sentences, over the themes 0 to (word 95th percentile + 5), starting flat. Each question uses the theme at the median of `postS`. The question types rotate: fill the blank (`mkBlank`), read and build the English (`mkBuild` mode `toEn`), listen and build (mode `listen`), build the sentence (mode `build`), then again. At least 4 and at most 6 questions (`PLACE.minS`, `PLACE.maxS`), same width rule.
3. **Sentences** come from `CUR[l][t].sents` (6 per theme). Tile types use sentences with 2 to 6 content tokens (`contentToks`); fill the blank needs a sentence that contains a word of theme t. If the theme has no fitting sentence, the next lower theme that has one is used and `q.unit` is set to it. A sentence is used once per session (`view.used`, by its English text). Distractors come from `poolFor(l, CUR[l][t].words)`. If no sentence fits at all, a meaning question is asked.
4. **Answer chances** (`PLACE.lk`, known theme / unknown theme). Words: correct 0.9 / 0.16, "I do not know" 0.03 / 0.42, wrong 0.07 / 0.42 (a guess of 1 in 4, made by about 2 in 3 of the learners who do not know the word). Fill the blank: correct 0.85 / 0.16, "I do not know" 0.03 / 0.42, wrong 0.12 / 0.42 (a slip is more likely than for a single word; tuned by simulation). Tile types (read and build the English, listen and build, build the sentence): correct 0.80 / 0.03, "I do not know" 0.04 / 0.55, wrong 0.16 / 0.42 (a correct answer is hardly ever a guess).
5. **Result.** The starting theme is the 10th percentile of `postS` (errs towards easier themes); the word 95th percentile is the upper limit. The result card shows: "Words: you know the words of about Themes 1 to X.", "Sentences: you can use the sentences of about Themes 1 to Y.", "Your starting theme is Theme Y+1, title.", the 90% range of the sentence result, the level name, and a rough-estimate note when fewer than 4 sentence questions were answered. Themes before the starting theme are marked as learned (box 3, due dates spread over 28 days).
6. **Screen.** Progress text "Placement quiz, part 1 of 2: words. Question N of about E" and "part 2 of 2: sentences"; sub-line "At most 14 questions. No timer." There is no screen between the parts and no auto-advance: after the last feedback of part 1 the learner taps Continue and part 2 starts with its header. "Finish now" (a link button on every question) ends the quiz: in part 1 with the word result only, in part 2 with the sentence result so far; both say rough estimate. Placement answers do not enter Mistakes (`misTrack` skips `view.s==="place"`).
7. **Back.** Back across the two parts shows the earlier answers. An answer changes the probability once (`q.upd`).
8. `placeOpenAt(type)` opens a screen directly for the phone test: `"blank"`, `"toEn"`, `"listen"`, `"build"` (a sentence question at Theme 1, part 2) or `"result"` (a fixed result).

Simulated learners (honest answers, 100 per position, seed fixed in the test): 11.4 to 11.5 questions on average, at most 14. The 90% range contains the true sentence position in 99 to 100 of 100 runs at positions 0, 6, 40, 100; at the last theme it is 89 (Chinese) and 69 (Japanese) of 100, because one slip on the last question moves the upper end by 1 theme; the test therefore allows 1 theme at the two ends. The starting theme is at or below the true position in 94 to 100 of 100 runs (at position 0 the start can be Theme 2, one theme too high). A learner with words up to Theme 100 but sentences up to Theme 40 starts at or below Theme 46 in 100 of 100 runs.
